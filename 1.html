About the series

Everything is connected.

Debuting in 2014 and spanning three mainline entries developed by Ubisoft Montreal and Ubisoft Toronto, Watch Dogs explores the friction between ubiquitous surveillance, corporate power, and digital freedom.

Each installment shifts to a new open-world metropolis—from Chicago and the San Francisco Bay Area to near-future London—letting players combine combat, stealth, drones, and real-time infrastructure hacking to outsmart the central operating system (ctOS).

Gameplay systems // ctOS toolkit

Core mechanics

Every mission is a sandbox where physical stealth and digital manipulation overlap.

01 // Infrastructure Control

City-Wide Hacking

Trigger city-wide blackouts, overload junction boxes, raise road blockers during high-speed pursuits, or hijack vehicles remotely—turning every connected street fixture into a tactical trap.

02 // Data & Recruitment

The Profiler

Scan any citizen on the street to uncover private texts, bank accounts, and daily schedules—evolving in Legion into a full recruitment system where any Londoner can join DedSec with unique perks.

03 // Remote Infiltration

Drones & RC Gadgets

Scout restricted compounds through security camera chains or deploy quadcopters, RC Jumpers, and Spiderbots to slip through air vents, unlock doors, and complete objectives without setting foot inside.

04 // Sandbox & Multiplayer

Playstyle & Invasions

Clear outposts as an unseen ghost, rely on non-lethal stun gear, or go loud with 3D-printed weapons—while seamless online Hacking Invasions let rival players sneak into your session to steal data.
